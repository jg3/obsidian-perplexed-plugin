## Why Care?

The opening of the plugin was aimed at venture-capital, private-equity, and equities-trading research drafts. Those three deep-research templates are no longer what this install should seed. The general research templates stay. The new default work is checking and rewriting a note you already have.

## What's New?

- **Stored workflows** seed into `obsidian-perplexed/workflows` and run from **Run stored workflow**. Ten review instructions append a result under the selection, or under the note when nothing is selected. Fact-check workflows request citations and a short recency window.
- **Capture a process** asks for a checking, editing, or modification process and writes a new workflow file. Existing filenames are not overwritten.
- **Market-map, market-category, and standards-and-specs** are no longer bundled. Copies already in a vault stay until you delete them.
- **Vault git.** After a successful template or workflow write, Perplexed reminds you to commit locally with Obsidian Git. A setting can run `obsidian-git:commit` instead. Remote push stays off unless **Also push to the remote** is enabled.
